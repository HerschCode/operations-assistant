# 1. Purpose
Governs inspection of incoming goods and handling of defects for all Purchase Orders received
by Northstar Manufacturing.

# 2. Incoming Inspection
Every delivery is inspected against the Purchase Order specification before the receiving dock
marks it complete. Inspection is sampling-based: a 10% sample for standard orders, 100% inspection
for any order from a supplier under Manual Credit Review (Procurement Policy Section 5) or any
supplier still in the Vendor Onboarding trial-order stage.

# 3. Defect Classification
Defects are classified Minor, Major, or Critical. A Critical defect (safety risk or complete
non-conformance to spec) triggers an automatic hold on the entire delivery and a Safety Incident
Report if the defect could have reached the production line undetected -- see Safety Incident
Reporting Procedure Section 2.

# 4. Rework vs. Return
A Minor or Major defect may be reworked on-site if the supplier agrees to cover rework cost and
the rework can complete within 3 business days without affecting the case's SLA target. Otherwise
the delivery is returned and treated as a Supplier Dispute under Exception Handling Procedure
Section 3 -- the SLA hold provisions there apply identically to a quality-driven dispute.

# 5. Defect Rate and Vendor Risk
A supplier whose Critical or Major defect rate exceeds 2% of deliveries in a rolling 90-day window
triggers an out-of-cycle Vendor Risk Score review (Vendor Onboarding Procedure Section 6), separate
from and in addition to the cycle-time-based trigger described there.

# 6. Corrective Action Requests
Any Critical defect requires a written Corrective Action Request from the supplier within 5
business days, reviewed by the Vendor Risk team before the supplier's next order is released.
