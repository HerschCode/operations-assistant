# 1. When to Escalate
A case should be escalated to Operations leadership when any of the following apply:
- The case has breached its SLA target (see SLA Policy) by more than 5 business days
- The case has undergone more than 2 rounds of PO Change Approval
- A supplier is unresponsive for more than 3 business days during Manual Credit Review

# 2. Escalation Path
Requesting department manager -> Procurement Team Lead -> Operations Director. Each level has
2 business days to act before automatic escalation to the next level.

# 3. What Escalation Does NOT Do
Escalation does not override the approval requirements in the Procurement Policy -- it accelerates
attention to a stalled case, it does not skip required approval steps.

# 4. Recurring Patterns
If the same supplier or business unit appears in escalations more than twice in a quarter,
Operations leadership should review that supplier/unit's process handling specifically, rather
than treating each escalation as an isolated incident.
