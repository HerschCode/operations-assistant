# 1. Purpose
Covers cases that fall outside the standard Procurement Policy flow.

# 2. Emergency Purchases
An emergency purchase (production-stopping shortage) may bypass standard requisition approval
with verbal authorization from a department manager, but must have a retroactive Purchase
Requisition filed within 2 business days. Emergency purchases are still subject to Manual Credit
Review if the supplier meets the criteria in Procurement Policy Section 5.

# 3. Supplier Disputes
If a supplier disputes an order's terms after PO release, the case is held (not cancelled) pending
resolution, and does not count toward SLA breach metrics during the hold period -- this is the one
documented exception to the SLA Policy's Section 3 rule that all process time counts.

# 4. Data Corrections
If a case's recorded activity sequence contains an obvious data-entry error (e.g. a duplicate
Approval event from a system glitch, not an actual second approval), Operations may flag it for
manual correction in the source system. This procedure does not authorize retroactively changing
what actually happened -- only correcting erroneous records of what happened.
