# 1. Purpose
Sets minimum retention periods and disposal rules for procurement and operational records.

# 2. Retention Periods
| Record type | Minimum retention |
|---|---|
| Purchase Orders and approvals | 7 years from final payment |
| Supplier contracts | 7 years after expiry or termination |
| Quality inspection records | 5 years |
| Vendor Risk Score history | 5 years |
| Operational event logs (process mining data) | 3 years |
| Emergency Purchase retroactive requisitions | 7 years (treated as Purchase Orders) |
| Safety incident reports and investigations | 10 years |

# 3. Legal Hold
Any record subject to a pending dispute (including a Supplier Dispute under Exception Handling
Procedure Section 3) or legal proceeding is exempt from disposal regardless of the age or retention
period in Section 2, until Legal releases the hold in writing.

# 4. Disposal
Electronic records are deleted using the approved secure-deletion tooling and logged in the disposal
register. Physical assets flagged obsolete under Inventory Management Policy Section 6 follow the
same register and require Compliance sign-off before disposal, not just Operations.

# 5. Data Corrections and Retention
A record corrected under Exception Handling Procedure Section 4 retains both the original erroneous
entry and the correction for the full retention period -- correction never replaces or deletes the
original, only annotates it.

# 6. Access Restrictions
Records containing supplier bank details or Vendor Risk Score inputs are restricted to Procurement
and Compliance staff. Operations may view aggregated risk tiers but not the underlying score inputs.
