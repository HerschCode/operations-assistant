# 1. Purpose
Defines how workplace safety incidents connected to procured materials are reported and investigated.

# 2. Reportable Incidents
An incident is reportable if it involves injury, a near-miss with potential for injury, or a
defective or non-conforming delivered material that could have reached the production line
undetected -- including any Critical defect under Quality Control Procedure Section 3.

# 3. Reporting Timeline
A reportable incident must be reported to the Safety Officer within 4 hours of discovery. A written
incident report must follow within 24 hours. Failure to meet either deadline is itself a reportable
compliance event, separate from the underlying incident.

# 4. Investigation
The Safety Officer leads a root-cause investigation, with Procurement and Quality Control
participation whenever a supplier or delivered material is implicated. Investigation must conclude
within 10 business days, and findings are shared with Procurement's Vendor Risk team for input to
the supplier's Vendor Risk Score (Vendor Onboarding Procedure Section 3).

# 5. Supplier Consequences
A supplier implicated in a safety incident is placed on immediate hold (no new Purchase Orders)
until the investigation concludes. If the investigation finds the supplier at fault, that supplier
is subject to Termination for Cause review under Contract Renewal Policy Section 6.

# 6. Records
Incident reports and investigation findings are retained for 10 years -- longer than any other
record type in the Data Retention Policy Section 2 -- because of potential long-tail liability.
