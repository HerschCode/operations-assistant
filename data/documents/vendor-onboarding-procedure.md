# 1. Purpose
Defines how a new supplier is qualified and added to Northstar Manufacturing's approved vendor
list before any Purchase Order can be issued to them.

# 2. Qualification Stages
A prospective supplier must clear three stages, in order: (1) financial/credit screening, (2)
compliance and insurance verification, (3) a trial order not exceeding $5,000. A supplier cannot
skip stages regardless of order urgency -- see Section 5 for the sole exception.

# 3. Vendor Risk Score
Every qualified supplier receives a Vendor Risk Score (0-100, lower is riskier) at onboarding,
recalculated quarterly thereafter. A score below 40 places the supplier under Manual Credit Review
for every order regardless of order value, per Procurement Policy Section 5 -- onboarding is where
that flag first gets set, not something Manual Credit Review decides independently.

# 4. Trial Order Requirements
The trial order (Stage 3) must be fulfilled on time and pass incoming Quality Control inspection
(see Quality Control Procedure Section 2) before the supplier is added to the approved vendor
list. A failed or late trial order requires a 90-day waiting period before re-application.

# 5. Emergency Onboarding Exception
A supplier may be conditionally approved for a single Emergency Purchase (Exception Handling
Procedure Section 2) without completing all three qualification stages, but the conditional
approval expires after that one order -- full qualification is still required before any
subsequent order.

# 6. Ongoing Monitoring
Approved suppliers with a cycle-time standard deviation more than double the category average
(see the companion operations-performance project's supplier performance metrics) are flagged for
Vendor Risk Score review ahead of the normal quarterly schedule.
